# AI Media Recommendation & Planning System

An AI-powered media recommendation and planning platform designed to help businesses make smarter marketing and media investment decisions based on their business objectives, industry, target audience, budget, location, and expected outcomes.

---

## 📌 Overview

Small and medium-sized businesses often struggle to decide:

- Where should they spend their marketing budget?
- Which media channels are best for their business?
- How much should they allocate to digital, print, events, outdoor, or other media?
- What kind of return can they reasonably expect?
- Which media channels are most relevant to their target audience?
- How should their marketing budget be distributed over a 3-month, 6-month, or 12-month period?

This project aims to solve these problems through an AI-powered media recommendation system.

The system collects information about a business, researches its industry and market environment, analyzes its objectives and target audience, and generates a customized media mix with estimated budget allocation and potential ROI.

> **Important:** ROI and performance figures generated by the system are estimates based on available data, assumptions, historical information, and industry benchmarks. They are not guaranteed financial or marketing outcomes.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Many small and medium-sized organizations do not have large marketing teams, dedicated media planners, or sufficient budgets to conduct extensive market research.

As a result, businesses may:

- Spend money on unsuitable media channels
- Allocate budgets without sufficient market research
- Choose media based only on assumptions
- Lack industry-specific marketing insights
- Have difficulty estimating potential ROI
- Fail to understand where their target customers actually consume media
- Struggle to create an effective long-term media plan

This application aims to make strategic media planning more accessible to businesses with limited marketing resources.

# 🤖 How AI Works

The AI workflow can be divided into several stages.

## Stage 1 — Collect Business Information

The system collects structured information through a form.

## Stage 2 — Understand the Business

The AI interprets:

- Business type
- Industry
- Business maturity
- Business objectives
- Vision
- Mission
- Growth plans
- Target customers

## Stage 3 — Research the Industry

The AI researches relevant industry information using reliable external sources.

## Stage 4 — Research the Company

The AI analyzes available information about the specific company.

## Stage 5 — Analyze Market & Geography

The AI evaluates the target city, region, market, and customer environment.

## Stage 6 — Analyze Media Opportunities

The system evaluates potential media channels based on the business context.

## Stage 7 — Analyze Budget

The AI considers the available marketing budget and campaign duration.

## Stage 8 — Build the Media Mix

The system recommends a combination of suitable media channels.

## Stage 9 — Allocate the Budget

The system estimates how the available budget could be distributed across selected channels.

All financial recommendations should be displayed in:

**₹ INR**

## Stage 10 — Estimate Performance

Depending on available data, the system may estimate:

- Reach
- Impressions
- Leads
- Conversions
- Customer Acquisition Cost
- Engagement
- Expected Revenue
- Estimated ROI

## Stage 11 — Generate the Final Recommendation

The system produces an easy-to-understand media planning report explaining:

- Recommended media
- Why each channel was selected
- Suggested budget allocation
- Campaign duration
- Expected outcomes
- Estimated ROI
- Assumptions
- Risks
- Alternative scenarios

# 📊 ROI & Scenario Planning

Instead of presenting one number as a guaranteed result, the system should ideally provide scenarios.

### Conservative Scenario

Lower expected performance based on cautious assumptions.

### Expected Scenario

Performance based on available benchmarks and relevant historical information.

### Optimistic Scenario

Higher performance under favorable assumptions.

This allows business owners to understand the potential range of outcomes.
