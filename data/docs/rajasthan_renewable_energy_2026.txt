RAJASTHAN RENEWABLE ENERGY LANDSCAPE

According to the source:

- Rajasthan ranks 2nd among Indian states in installed renewable energy
  capacity, with a total of 28,617 MW.

- Rajasthan ranks 1st in solar power with an installed capacity of
  22,860.73 MW.

- Rajasthan ranks 3rd in wind power with an installed capacity of
  5,195.82 MW.

- The source states that Rajasthan registered the highest annual growth
  among major states at 18.63% in installed renewable energy capacity.

- Rajasthan's solar generation potential is assessed at 142 GW.

- Rajasthan receives high solar radiation, averaging approximately
  6-7 kWh/m²/day.

- The source identifies Jaisalmer, Barmer and Jodhpur as areas with
  suitable conditions for wind-energy projects.

INFRASTRUCTURE AND CLUSTERS

The source identifies the following renewable-energy infrastructure
and clusters:

- Green Energy Corridor in western Rajasthan.
- Bhadla Solar Park Cluster.
- Jaisalmer Wind Energy Cluster.
- Barmer Solar & Wind Energy Cluster.
- Phalodi-Pokhran Solar Cluster.
- Pugal and Khuiyala Solar Cluster in Bikaner district.

The source states that Rajasthan has a fully operational solar park
spanning 5,700 hectares, including the 2,245 MW Solar Park in Bhadla.

POLICIES AND INCENTIVES

The source references the following Rajasthan policies and support:

1. Rajasthan Investment Promotion Scheme (RIPS) 2024

For renewable energy plants, the source states:

- Investment subsidy of 75% of State tax due and deposited for a period
  of 7 years.
- 100% exemption from electricity duty for 7 years.
- 100% conversion-charge benefits in stages as notified by the State.
- The State may promote development of solar plants through joint
  ventures with private developers.

The source also describes incentives for solar PV module and cell
manufacturing, including capital subsidy / turnover-linked incentive
options, electricity-duty exemption, stamp-duty and conversion-charge
exemptions, employment boosters, training and skilling support, and
other incentives subject to the applicable policy conditions.

2. Rajasthan Renewable Energy Policy, 2023

The source lists:

- Exemption from transmission and distribution charges for renewable
  energy projects.
- Waiver of cross-subsidy surcharges for renewable energy.
- Reduction in electricity duty on solar energy from 60 paisa per unit
  to 40 paisa per unit.
- 100% exemption on land tax for renewable energy projects for seven
  years.
- Waiver of banking charges for electricity generated from renewable
  sources.

3. Rajasthan Green Hydrogen Policy, 2023

The source lists incentives including:

- Transmission and distribution charge exemption for qualifying
  facilities.
- Cross-subsidy surcharge exemption for qualifying green hydrogen
  procurement.
- Land allotment at competitive rates.
- Waiver of banking charges for electricity generated from renewable
  sources.

4. Rajasthan Biomass and Waste to Energy Policy, 2023

The source lists:

- Subsidies and grants for qualifying plants.
- Land at concessional rates.
- Tax and duty exemptions for specified periods.
- Priority in grid connectivity.
- Financial assistance for research and development.
